Three-panel schematic comparing how text reaches an image-generating network. Panels are side by side, left to right.
Panel (a) 'Encoder + diffusion transformer': at the bottom two input boxes 'text tokens' and 'noisy image tokens'. 'text tokens' has an arrow up into a gray box 'text encoder (separate model)'. One arrow labeled 'last-layer features only' goes from the text encoder into a tall blue box 'diffusion transformer, N layers'. 'noisy image tokens' has an arrow up into the same blue box. One output arrow at the top of the blue box labeled 'velocity'.
Panel (b) 'Single tower': one tall blue box 'shared transformer, N layers' containing the small note 'one set of weights for all tokens'. At the bottom three small input boxes 'text', 'video', 'audio', each with an arrow up into the box. At the top two output arrows labeled 'video velocity' and 'audio velocity'.
Panel (c) 'Dual tower (MoT)': two tall boxes side by side: left orange box 'understanding tower (frozen)', right blue box 'generation tower'. 'text tokens' has an arrow up into the left box, 'noisy image tokens' has an arrow up into the right box. Exactly three short horizontal arrows at different heights go from the left box to the right box, with the single label 'K, V at every layer'. There are no arrows from the right box to the left box. One output arrow at the top of the right box labeled 'velocity'.
